# P15 — Plan active phrases and stable lanes

**Size:** 5. **Prerequisite:** P14.

## Work

- Sweep validated phrase starts and ends to produce deterministic screen states. Apply ends and starts at the same tick as one transition. Keep each continuing phrase in its lane when another enters or clears.
- Use neutral labels where they clarify independently supported ownership. Preserve an unknown owner where evidence is insufficient. Treat extra readable voices as a crowding condition and retain their text. Record combined reading demand without stretching cue timing.

## Acceptance

- Assert A alone → A+B → B alone → clear, stable B placement, same-onset overlap, coincident end and start, and a third intelligible voice. Check repeatability when the same inputs arrive in a different order.
