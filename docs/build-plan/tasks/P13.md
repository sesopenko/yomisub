# P13 — Reconcile wording and speaker evidence

**Size:** 8. **Prerequisite:** P12.

## Work

- Keep the continuous reading as the starting candidate. Link retries and stem hypotheses as alternatives. Record corroboration, disagreement and the reasons a candidate is selected or left unresolved. Keep confirmed listener corrections as an independent overlay and retain the original observations.
- Use an in-bounds candidate span from the source observation to propose ownership. An exclusively covering voice is provisional until P14 checks the final interval. For a phrase continuing into overlap, require a timed lexical portion of that same phrase with exclusive coverage by one voice, or an explicit listener mapping. Store its evidence ID. A matching mix and stem reading can support wording while leaving ownership open.
- Emit candidates and their alternatives for P14. Keep disputed primary wording at the continuous reading with a review finding, unless an explicit listener correction resolves it. Supported primary words with valid timing may display without an owner label. Keep a proposed second voice review-only until P14 validates its text, owner and final interval. Retain wordless voices as activity findings.

## Acceptance

- Verify unknown ownership even when mix and stem match but two voices remain plausible. Check a continuing solo-to-overlap phrase, a recurrent voice, a wordless second voice, and an A/B/B correction over the S01E03 merged cluster. Assert rejected alternatives and automatic observations are unchanged after corrections.

- Match overlapping retry candidates to the same recording-clock occurrence before considering text. A candidate wholly in padding cannot add another line for that core. Exercise a supported second phrase with matching mix/stem wording, a unique timed solo continuation and independently timed edges. Preserve a genuinely repeated utterance at a distinct time.
