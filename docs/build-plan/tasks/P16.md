# P16 — Serialize SRT and ASS

**Size:** 5. **Prerequisite:** P15.

## Work

- Serialize SRT as successive intervals of the active text, ending the last cue when speech clears. Serialize ASS as independently timed entries with fixed lanes. Both consume the same planned timing and wording.
- Quantize at the format boundary. Handle intervals that collapse at export precision without emitting zero-length events or moving neighboring cues. Keep any lost visibility reviewable. Serialize UTF-8 and ensure dialogue cannot be interpreted as ASS control markup.

## Acceptance

- Parse both outputs and compare them to the source plan within export precision. Test Unicode, literal markup, adjacent intervals, collapsed intervals and clear events. Render the A/B fixture with FFmpeg/libass and inspect the continuing phrase before and after the other clears.

- Convert each shared boundary once per format, to the nearest millisecond for SRT and centisecond for ASS, rounding half ticks up. Omit a quantized zero-duration interval in that format and return a finding with its phrase/state IDs for P17. Test opposite sides of a half tick, two events sharing a tick and one event visible in SRT that collapses in ASS.

- Verify a rendered three-voice fixture contains three distinct readable phrases. Clamp exported ticks to the representable playable interval, then check for collapse. Route collapse diagnostics into the returned findings and test that one lost phrase referenced by several states has one finding for that format.

## Sources

- FFmpeg/libass held the continuing lane stable in the known-time test. SRT conveys changing active text and its placement varies by player.
