## Work

- Compare the P04 frozen legacy SRT with the rebuilt automatic SRT using the same selected streams and medium baseline checkpoint. Score timing and phrase recovery on these displayed files. Check ASS text and timing parity against the cue plan within its format precision. Score ownership and detected coactivity from the run evidence, and verify assigned labels in the exports. Keep evaluation references out of automatic inputs. Score corrected replay separately.
- Exercise all research cases below with the annotations frozen in P04. Record input and track identity, model and runtime provenance, output or evidence locations and per-scene measurements. Score control activity against the known source placement and phrase timing against independently verified speech edges. Keep new or disputed observations outside the frozen comparison.
- Through GTK, generate all three outputs for a short language-tagged multitrack video using `tiny`, then replay its bundle offline. Record AMD Vulkan and ROCm devices, stage wall times and peak memory where measured. Exercise two physical GPUs when available and label simulated placement as such. Report clip measurements in their measured scope.

## Acceptance

- Use the frozen P04 reference IDs and matching rules. Require every verified phrase recovered by legacy to be recovered again. For the fixed, nonempty baseline-recovered subset with verified edges, compute paired absolute onset and release errors in milliseconds from the displayed subtitles. Require one median to decrease and the other to stay equal or decrease. Publish the per-ID measurements, eligible counts, misses and unscored cases. New matches cannot compensate for losing a required phrase.
- Require automatic attribution of the two checked S01E01 speakers under the frozen recording-wide correspondence. Require the detector to match the known event on both the same-onset and staggered controls under the P04 event rule, and to report none on the alternating control. Check supported overlapping phrases in both exports through their independent entries and releases. Label the evidence used for that display as automatic, corrected or fixture-based.
- Record pass, fail or unverified for each release check, with automatic outputs scored before corrections. The natural-scene observations characterize failure modes where reference wording, ownership or edges are unknown. A corrected or fixture display can verify rendering, while the automatic speaker and detector gates require an automatic run.
- Rerun the standard gates and add a regression for every defect resolved here. If a gate fails, leave it open and follow the plan’s correction and sizing rules.
