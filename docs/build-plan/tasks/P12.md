# P12 — Propose Japanese phrase and line breaks

**Size:** 5. **Prerequisite:** P11.

## Work

- Adapt the earlier Sudachi approach as the Japanese grouping strategy. Map exact Unicode surface spans onto the unwrapped Whisper segment and its lexical pieces. Reconstruct the original text exactly, including leading spaces and repeated surfaces. Keep segment-level grouping available in other supported languages.
- Propose linguistically useful boundaries with exact offsets. Keep particles with their phrases and negation or contractions with their predicates. A display wrap may fall inside an ASR piece. A timed break becomes a candidate for P14 only where usable edge evidence can be located.
- Fall back to unsplit segment text when the lexical join fails and report the missing evidence. Preserve wording, register and spelling disputes.

## Acceptance

- Test repeated surfaces, leading spaces, a piece spanning multiple characters, mismatch fallback, a wrap inside a piece, and the non-Japanese strategy. Check that `できない`, `食べちゃった` and a late negative remain intact at proposed timed cuts.
